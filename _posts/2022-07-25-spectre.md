when i feel breath as labour

quenching the angst in drops

spectre creeps in as always.



near a window by the sea

sitting on a thorny cushion

faded into pain's delight 

two eyes a pair of ears

drove a ceaseless mind

Oh Boy! it was

burning candle in the window.
