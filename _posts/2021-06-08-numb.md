when someone

who felt everything a little too much

begins to feel nothing.



cached into a swollen muscle

affecting irregular beatings in caution

lacking any release of pressure

blood churned in potency.

although for him, it seemed, he ran cold.

Sunny as with Pole

Oft he remained hot 

novel was to be cold,

without any chill, Hell.
